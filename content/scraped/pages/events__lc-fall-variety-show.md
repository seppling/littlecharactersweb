Our classes have been working hard this semester on a LOT of fun stuff, and we are so excited to present it to the public at our Fall Variety Show in November!

Performance Times:

-   Saturday, November 23 at 6pm
    
-   Sunday, November 24 at 3pm
    

Performers include our Scenes and Monologues Class, Middle School + Intermediate Improv Classes, Theater Games class and our Film class! There will also be opportunities for audience members to get onstage and play some games!

Tickets are $15 a piece (kids 4 and under are free!) and are on sale now!
