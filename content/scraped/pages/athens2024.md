## Spring 2025 Athens Classes

We are excited to offer after-school classes In Athens on Tuesdays and Wednesdays for students aged 3-17 this Spring this Spring from January 13th through May 16th! _While Hannah can’t teach every week this semester, she’ll be popping in 1-2 times a month in all of our Athens classes!_

Classes are located at [Work.Shop](https://www.workshopathens.com/) off of Chase Street. Although the reception area is small, we are minutes away from restaurants and coffee shops such as Maepole, Buvez, Sunroof Coffee and Sips!

To enroll in a class, click the below registration link and create an account on our online portal. You’ll be able to see all the classes available to your student once logged in! [Please click here to see our tuition guidelines and discounts!](/s/Little-Characters-Theater-Troupe-Tuition-Guidelines-2025.pdf) Feel free to reach out if you have any questions!

**The last day to register for Produce a Show classes is** **February 1st** **and the last day to register for all other classes is** **March 1st****!**

### Wednesday Classes

-   ## Intro To Theater
    
    **4:15-5:00pm | 6-10 Year Olds**
    
    $85/month + $30 performance fee
    
    This course will broaden your Little Characters’ view of all things theater! We will cover improv, movement, character development and more while working to build confidence, teamwork and individualism!
    
    Teachers: CC Conner, Anna Nelson
    
-   ## Intermediate Improv
    
    **5:15-6:15pm | 8-13 Year Olds**
    
    $100/month + $15 Performance Fee
    
    Have some experience with Improv and want to dive deeper? This class is for you. We will dig into storytelling, rhyming, puns and more! Level 2 class - must have some prior experience in Improv.
    
    Teacher: CC Conner, Peyton Harris
    
-   ## Produce a Show! - 8-13 Year Olds
    
    **6:15 - 7:30pm | 8-13 Year Olds**
    
    $120/month + $50 performance fee
    
    This class is for creative minds and mini producers! We will work with the students to create their own story, turn it into a script, and spend the semester rehearsing to bring it to life at the end of the semester.
    
    Teachers: CC Conner, Anna Nelson
