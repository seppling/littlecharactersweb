CCSD Half Days call for extra fun for kids while parents still get to work! 

We are so excited to partner with an **Improv Class at UGA** and **NEA's Big Read Program** to offer a special workshop that includes a free book and a performance opportunity! 

We will spend the afternoon learning about improv, creating scenes and playing games based on the theme of "Home", inspired by the book **Show Way by Jacqueline Woodson.** 

Each student will get to bring home a free book and will get to perform improv at UGA's Georgia Museum of Art family day on November 9th! 

**Ages: 6-14 (kids will be placed in different groups based on age!)** 

**When: 10/09 from 2-5pm**

**Where: Work.Shop Athens (off of Chase st!)**

**Cost: $30**
