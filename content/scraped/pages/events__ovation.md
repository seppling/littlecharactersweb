We’re thrilled to partner with [It’s Good to See You Productions](https://www.igtsyproductions.com/) to offer a monthly class for people with special needs! Hosted in [Brella Studio’s](https://brellastudio.com/) space, this hour will be spent playing games, allowing exploration through movement and costumes, and really just providing a fun and safe environment for our differently-abled friends and caregivers to enjoy! The cost will be $15 a class, but scholarship options are available.

Our tentative schedule is from 10-11am on:

-   August 26
    
-   September 23
    
-   October 21
    
-   November 18
    
-   December 16
