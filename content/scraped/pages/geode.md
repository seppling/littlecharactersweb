### For when you’re still a character, but not so little, we welcome you to **Geode.**

Beauty surprises us when we crack open our exterior and reveal what’s inside. While we all have little characters within, Geode exists to cater to our **tween, teen and adult populations** through classes, events, private lessons and more!

The hope of Geode is to provide fun, community-building experiences for tweens, teens and adults through theater without the commitment of nightly rehearsals.

Been writing a script and you want to get people together to read it? Let’s do it! Pipe dreams of a show? Let’s make it happen. Could your work environment use some enrichment through an improv for communications workshop? We got you.

Click the button below for inquiries and scroll down to see what events we have coming up!

Geode strives to deepen social and emotional health for teens, tweens and adults in a safe, fun environment through the art of theater.

## Geode used to be just for adults, but we’re opening our arms a little wider now.

**Since its start in 2022, Little Characters has worked with students of all ages and abilities. At the time, we had mostly young kids, and parents who wanted to try it for themselves.**

**As we’ve grown, our little kids have turned into kids and our kids have turned into big kids, and big kids don’t always like being called little.**

**So, starting in Summer 2026, we are changing the face of Geode to include kids 11 and up! We will still have adult only programs, and we will still have 11-17 programs, but they will fall under the name _Geode_.**

**And, how cool will it be if in the future, we can mix these populations to put on family friendly shows represented by actors the ages of their characters? Join us as we dare to dream.**

#### Yes, And… Improv Class

**Mondays |** **4:30-5:30pm | Ages: 8+**

$95/month + $20 performance fee

In improv, "Yes, And..." means accepting an idea and building on it—and that's exactly what this class is all about!

Through fast-paced games, creative storytelling, and unscripted scenes, students will grow their confidence, creativity, and collaboration skills in a fun, supportive environment. Whether they're new to improv or experienced performers, every class is full of laughter, surprises, and endless possibilities. Your student might just learn that Yes, And works outside of theater, too. ;)
