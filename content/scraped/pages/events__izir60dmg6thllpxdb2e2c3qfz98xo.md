This three day intensive camp will focus on improv techniques and writing our own performance **for 11-17 year olds**. Camp will run from 9am-1pm.

$150 covers the week, snacks and materials, just bring a lunch! Option for after-camp from 1-3 on the 5th and 6th for $30 a day.

We will spend time learning improv techniques and writing our own variation of a popular story. Putting together set pieces, costumes and props all leading up to a performance for parents and friends at 12:30 on Friday!

Camp will take place at the Winterville Campus for Arts and Culture located at 371 N. Church St. Winterville, GA 30683.

_We always want to make our camps accessible to everyone, so please reach out if you are interested in a scholarship!_
