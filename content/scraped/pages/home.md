# **Encouraging Creative Hearts Through Theater.**

Every person has a story worth telling. At **Little Characters Theater Troupe**, we use theater to help children, teens, and adults discover confidence, creativity, and a place where they belong.

Through dramatic play, improvisation, storytelling, and script writing, we foster creative confidence through engaging, student-centered theater experiences.

* * *

* * *

## **Our Approach**

-   ![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61fc0808d7415f6a4cd36b0a/f22c8933-5751-48fa-be74-6c9bd6e4c69d/IMG_1625.jpg)
    
    ## Meet You Where You're At
    
    Theater is an expression of the human condition. We are all born beautiful individuals with differing gifts. We strive to meet our students where they are, whether it’s nervous or excited, first-timers or masters of the stage. We get to know each little character and help them feel comfortable and curious to learn.
    
-   ![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61fc0808d7415f6a4cd36b0a/67445bf5-9e8b-4d99-b0bd-f6884abb8592/IMG_1498.jpeg)
    
    ## Learn and Rehearse
    
    We get to know each other and foster a team environment through games, exercises and trust experiences. Then, build confidence, express emotions and grow communication skills by working on scenes, scripts and character development.
    
-   ![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61fc0808d7415f6a4cd36b0a/80ab8bb3-b9ec-4805-82f5-fe400ef07c69/20230520_190322.jpg)
    
    ## Encourage and Perform!
    
    Rehearsing with friends is one thing, but performing in front of others can be intimidating! We foster an environment of encouragement by sharing affirmations with each other, and one-on-one prep time with our teachers. It’s a gift to showcase our hard work for our loved ones!
    

# What We Offer

-   #### Classes
    
    We are happy to offer classes to students ages 4-100 through Little Characters and Geode. Our programs allow students young and old a place to cultivate their creative confidence a sense of belonging through various subjects!
    
-   #### Summer Camp
    
    Each summer, we offer 3-5 weeks of camp for students 4-17. From performing an original show to Improv and Technical Theater, there is something for all of your Little Characters! Check it out!
    
-   #### Special Needs Accomodations
    
    All of our staff have experience working with kids of all ages and abilities. It is a treasure to be able to introduce theater to our friends with diverse needs and we work with parents to make sure everyone feels safe and welcome!
    
    We are now officially partners with the UGA Speech and Hearing clinic, and have the ability to partner any students with higher needs up with a class “buddy” at no additional cost.
    
-   #### A Dynamic Staff
    
    Our teachers come from varied backgounds and are masters in their fields! We are so grateful to have such a robust team! [Meet them all here!](/team)
    

## **Other Services**

* * *

#### Private Lessons

Looking for audition help, assistance in playwriting, one-on-one acting, or singing lessons? We provide private lessons on an hourly basis.

#### Workshops + Mini-Camps

We hold weekend workshops 1-2 times a semester, as well as half-day workshops and mini-camps on CCSD school holidays.

#### Local School + Community Classes

Little Characters is verified as a CCSD school vendor. We offer after-school programs and weekend events throughout the county. We are currently running programs at Athens Montessori School, Oglethorpe Ave. Elementary School, and Love.Craft Athens.

#### Adult Programming

Our sister company, [Geode](/geode), exists to excite adult imaginations through classes, workshops and private lessons. Geode strives to deepen social and emotional health in a safe, fun environment through the art of theater.

#### Parties

Now that we have our own space, we offer customizable parties for any occasion! Check out our [FAQ page](/faqs) to learn more.
