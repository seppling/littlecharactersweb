Did October sneak up on you?! Are your kids begging you to buy a costume and you’d rather have something homemade but you can’t find the time or energy? Don’t worry - we’ve got you. Our magnificent costumers, Maddie and CC are offering a day full of costuming fun on October 14th!

Have your kid bring a costume at any phase - a picture of an idea, a half-thought out creation, or a full blown costume that just needs some flair! We will have fabric, decor, paint, sewing materials and the works to help get these costumes from drab to fab.

Each student will also get to make a custom candy tote bag!

Open to kids and teens of all ages!

Monday, October 14th from 12:30-4:30pm. $75 per student. Snacks and materials included!
