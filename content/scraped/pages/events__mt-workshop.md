Come be the king (or queen) of New York in this fun ensemble dance workshop! We will be mixing the trifecta of music, dance, and acting to put together a high-energy number from the hit Broadway musical Newsies! Who doesn’t love to dance and sing?

It is highly recommended that students bring closed-toed, active shoes that they can move around in, general activewear for dancing, and a water bottle and snack for fueling up/cooling down.

Please fill out the form below to register! Sean will be in touch with details on parking and such the days before!
