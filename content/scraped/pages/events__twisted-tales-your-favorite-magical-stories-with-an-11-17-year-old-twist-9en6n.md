Remember your favorite fairy tales? Yes, well, we took those and jazzed 'em up a bit by letting our 7-10 year old students retell them THEIR way. With interpretations of Jack in the Beanstalk, The Three Little Pigs, Snow White and The Wizard of Oz, this night holds a story for everyone!

We've loved each and every minute spent with our Little Characters students this semester in our biggest semester yet! We can not wait to show you all of the hard work, creativity and just pure joy these kids have put into the shows.

This show will have 4 original performances (at around 15 mins each!) with intermissions in-between! Snacks provided and drinks for purchase!

May 4th - 6:30PM

Tickets are $13/person - kids 3 and under are free!
