We are inviting students 7 and up to come spend the Tuesday morning of Thanksgiving Break singing, dancing, acting out their favorite parts of Kpop Demon Hunters! Mr. Sean will lead us in singing "Golden", Miss. Sosebee will teach us the upbeat dance to "Soda Pop!" and Ms. Hannah will help us tackle monologues from the movie! 

While the performing part is fun, our biggest emphasis of the day will be helping instill confidence in your students by helping them create personalized Affirmation Posters that they can put in a special place at home to embrace how awesome each and every one of them are! 

We ask our students to wear closed toed shoes and clothes they can move around in! Nut-free snacks will be provided, but please bring a water bottle! 

The Important Information: 

**Ages: 7+**

**When: 11/25 from 9am-noon**

**Where: 1635 W. Broad St. Athens, GA 30606 in the Upstairs Studio!**

**Cost: $50**
