Fall Break is upon us and what a better way to celebrate than with a storybook mini-camp?!

We will play games, create props and costumes and act out some of our favorite books: **Dragons Love Tacos** and **It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown**

Camp will run from 9-1pm on Thursday, October 10 and Friday October 11 at Work.Shop off of Chase Street!

$50 per day, feel free to sign up for one day or both!

Kids ages 4-10 welcome. There will be a short performance for parents at 12:45 each day!
