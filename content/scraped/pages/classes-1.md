## Fall 2026 Classes!

**Registration is now OPEN for all classes!**

We are excited to offer classes for students ages 4-17 this school year! Classes are divided by age range and will run from August 17 - December 18. _The Produce a Show class and Performance Troupe class will run through March 31, 2027._ All classes will be at our location near downtown Athens - 1635 W Broad St. Athens, GA 30606! **Scroll down to see all of our offerings, and don’t forget to read the details below!**

All of our classes offer a deep enrichment model of 7 students to 1 teacher, and classes are capped at 20 students or less, depending on the subject matter! **We do everything we can to ensure we cater to the specific needs of all of our students**, and rich communication with parents is key to Little Characters! _We also are partnering with the UGA Speech and Hearing clnic to offer 1-1 support for students with higher needs!_

**New to Little Characters? Your first class is always free!** All classes can be paid for up front for the semester, or in five monthly installments. [Please click here to see our tuition guidelines and discounts](/s/Fall-2026-LCTT-Tuition-Guidelines.pdf).

We are aware that some of these classes have wide age ranges, and we work diligently to ensure that students are met with age-appropriate material. **All classes will have a mid-semester showcase and will end with a big show at the end of the term!**

### Monday Classes

-   ## Produce a Show
    
    **5:45-7:00pm | Ages 7+**
    
    $115/month + $50 performance fee
    
    Produce a Show is where imagination takes center stage! Students work together to dream up an original story, create unforgettable characters, explore the world of technical theater, and turn their ideas into a fully staged production.
    
    From brainstorming and script writing to set design, costumes, and performance, every student plays an important role in bringing the story to life. Whether they love being in the spotlight or behind the scenes, this class celebrates creativity, collaboration, and the magic of creating something entirely your own and completely original.
    
    Teachers: CC Conner and Zack Newcott
    
    _\*This class is August - March_
    
    [**Register Now!**](https://app.thestudiodirector.com/littlecharacterstheater/portal.sd)

We will help nurture the physical and emotional development of your growing child all while having a whole lot of fun. The class will end with a performance at our Variety Show in December!

### Wednesday Classes

-   ## Yes, And… Improv Class
    
    **4:30-5:30pm | Ages: 8+**
    
    $95/month + $20 performance fee
    
    In improv, "Yes, And..." means accepting an idea and building on it—and that's exactly what this class is all about!
    
    Through fast-paced games, creative storytelling, and unscripted scenes, students will grow their confidence, creativity, and collaboration skills in a fun, supportive environment. Whether they're new to improv or experienced performers, every class is full of laughter, surprises, and endless possibilities. Your student might just learn that Yes, And works outside of theater, too. ;)
    
    Teacher: CC Conner
    
    [**Register Now!**](https://app.thestudiodirector.com/littlecharacterstheater/portal.sd)
    
-   ## LC/Geode Performance Troupe
    
    **5:45-7:00pm | Ages 9+**
    
    $115/month + $50 performance fee
    
    Have a student who is itching to perform more than once a semester? This class is for students that have worked in theater before, and want to sharpen their acting skills. We will spend the Fall putting on short performances around town, and then use December-March to prepare for a full length production!
    
    Students will dig deep into characterization through pre-written short plays, scenes and monologues, and learn more advanced improv techniques. Oh, and still have a lot of fun doing so!
    
    Teachers: Hannah Eppling, CC Conner
    
    _\*This class is August - March_
    
    [**Register Now!**](https://app.thestudiodirector.com/littlecharacterstheater/portal.sd)
