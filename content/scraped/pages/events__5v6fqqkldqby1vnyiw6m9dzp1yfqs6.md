A Second annual collaboration with Little Characters and TREEHOUSE kid and craft!

Thursday 10/10 and Friday 10/11 10am-4pm 

for kids ages 6-10 _(and for older kiddos who wish to attend too!)_

instructors:  CC Connor (Little Characters) and Shaunia Grant and Maypop Wren (TREEHOUSE kid and craft)

In celebration of Halloween we are pulling inspiration from this creative movie and have created a camp!  Kids will have fun whether they have seen the movie or not and we must mention that we are focusing on the silly and creative spirit of the movie.  We are aware that some children could be scared by some parts and are very aware of focusing the fun spirit of halloween and beetlejuice.  

We will be playing acting and improvisational games as well as creating props, costumes, art and working on a performance.  This is going to be a fun 2 day fall break camp.  

There will be a small performance at 3:45 on Friday at the end of camp.
