Got the Post-Holiday Blues? Come join us for a Winter Break mini camp to spice up the last week before heading back to school!

Kids will be separated into age groups and older kids will have the ability to assist with younger kids along with fun games and activities for themselves.

January 2, 9am-1:00pm, bring a packed lunch!

Cost is $140 for all three days or drop in daily for $50 each day!

Kids 4-10 will be campers, and kids 11+ kids will be mini counselors alongside our adult teachers! Camp will be located across from Chase Street Elementary School.

Please reach out if you’re interested in learning about scholarships.
