Get ready to catch a fun mix of acts and surprises in the Little Characters Spring 2026 Variety Show!

Prepare yourselves for a night of laughter and entertainment! Our classes have been working hard this semester on a LOT of fun stuff, and we are so excited to present our fourth bi-annual VARIETY SHOW!

Performers include the following classes:

Intermediate Improv, Film Creation Classes, Ovation Adaptive Theater Class, Music and Movement, and ABCs of Theater!

Saturday, May 16th at 6pm and Sunday, May 17th at 3pm.

Tickets are $12 a piece (kids 4 and under are free!)

Doors will open at 5:30 on Saturday and 2:30 on Sunday. There will be a raffle open to fundraise for future programming, and food and beverages available for purchase before the show and during intermission!
