We are excited to announce that our Performance Troupe will be performing at The State Botanical Garden of Georgia’s Insectival Festival on September 27th!

This day will offer tons of family fun and our performance of **_Jack and the Beanstalk_** is just one part of it! Our performance will take place in the Theater in the Woods stage, which is in the children’s garden at 1:30pm. Get there a little early to get a good seat!

Tickets for the festival can be purchased day-of at the garden entrance and are $5 per person, $20 per family, with 2 and under free. No additional cost for performance.

[Find out more about the festival here!](https://botgarden.uga.edu/event/35th-annual-insectival/)
