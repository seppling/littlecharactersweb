**That One Time We Put On a Show: Little Characters Film Screening!**

Come join us for a night of fun at the **Marigold Auditorium for Arts and Culture**! We'll be screening a selection of short films created by our Little Characters’ first ever Film 101 Class.

The audience will enjoy refreshments as they watch these short films and have the chance to ask the students questions about their films! Grab some popcorn, sit back, and enjoy the show with friends and family.

$7 per person, kids 3 and younger are free!
