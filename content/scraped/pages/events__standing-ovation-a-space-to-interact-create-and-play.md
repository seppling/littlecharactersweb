We are so excited to partner with [It’s Good to See You Productions](https://www.igtsyproductions.com) to offer our first class for children with special needs! Hosted in [Brella Studio’s](https://brellastudio.com) space, this hour will be spent playing games, allowing exploration through movement and costumes, and really just providing a fun and safe environment for our differently-abled friends and caregivers to enjoy!
