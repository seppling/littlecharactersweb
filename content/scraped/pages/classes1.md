## Weekly Classes!

We are excited to offer after-school classes for students aged 3-17 this upcoming Spring! Classes are divided by age range and will run from January 13th through May 16th.

All of our classes offer a deep enrichment model of 7 students to 1 teacher, and classes are capped at 20 students or less, depending on the subject matter! We do everything we can to ensure we cater to the specific needs of all of our students, and rich communication with parents is key to Little Characters!

**New to Little Characters? Your first class is always free!** All classes can be paid for up front for the semester, or in five monthly installments. We offer a 15% sibling discount and a 15% multiple class discount to help families who are interested in more than one class per week! It is our hope to offer classes to all students, so please let us know if you are interested in scholarships!

**Spring 2025 Registration is NOW OPEN! Scroll down to see our offerings, and** [**contact us**](/contact) **with any questions!**
