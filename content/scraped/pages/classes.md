## Spring 2026 Classes!

**Registration is now closed for all classes! Fall Class info will be up by July 24!**

We are excited to offer classes for students of all ages this Spring! Classes are divided by age range and will run from January 12-May 22. **All classes will be at our new location - 1635 W Broad St. Athens, GA 30606!**

All of our classes offer a deep enrichment model of 7 students to 1 teacher, and classes are capped at 20 students or less, depending on the subject matter! We do everything we can to ensure we cater to the specific needs of all of our students, and rich communication with parents is key to Little Characters!

**New to Little Characters? Your first class is always free!** All classes can be paid for up front for the semester, or in five monthly installments.[Please click here to see our tuition guidelines and discounts!](/s/Little-Characters-Theater-Troupe-Tuition-Guidelines-2026.pdf)

  
All classes will have a mid-semester showcase and will end with a big show at the end of the semester!

[Access Account to Register or Pay Tuition](https://app.thestudiodirector.com/littlecharacterstheater/portal.sd)

[Any questions? Email us!](/contact)

### Monday Classes

-   ## ABC’s of Theater
    
    **3:45-4:30pm | Ages 4-6**
    
    $85/month + $30 performance fee
    
    This class is for the littlest characters who show a lot of interest in performing and playing pretend! Come along with us on a journey through the alphabet to learn all about the theater while integrating games, activities, and songs!
    
    Teacher: Peyton Harris
    
-   ## Intro to Theater
    
    **4:30-5:15pm | Ages 7-10**
    
    $85/month + $30 performance fee
    
    This course will broaden your Little Characters' view of all things theater! We will cover vocality, movement, character exploration onstage while working individually and in groups to build confidence, teamwork and individualism!
    
    Teacher: CC Conner
    
-   ## Produce a Show
    
    **5:30-6:45pm | Ages 8-13**
    
    $115/month + $50 performance fee
    
    This class is for creative minds and mini producers! We will work with the students to create their own story, turn it into a script, and spend the semester preparing to bring it to life at the end of the semester. Got a student that is more interested in backstage work? Students can choose how much they want to spend time in the spotlight or working it, as we are combining acting and technical theater forces this semester!
    
    Teachers: CC Conner and Rotating Assistant Teachers
    

### Tuesday Classes

-   ## Music and Movement
    
    **4:15-5:00pm | Ages 4-6**
    
    $85/month + $20 performance fee
    
    Come have your little one experience the joy and liveliness of combining music and movement in this lively, engaging class! This class will use the power of singing, dancing, and pretending to build early skills in rhythm and harmony and nurture the physical and emotional development of your growing child.
    
    Teacher: Sean Wilson
    

### Wednesday Classes

-   ## Intermediate Improv
    
    **4:15-5:15pm | 8-12 year olds**
    
    $95/month + $25 performance fee
    
    Have some experience with Improv and want to dive deeper? This class is for you. We will dig deeper into characters, explore scene work, rhyming, puns and more! Level 2 class - must have some prior experience in Improv.
    
    Teacher: Peyton Harris
    
-   ## Ovation! Adaptive Theater Class
    
    **5:30-6:15pm | 6-15 Year Olds**
    
    $85/month + $30 Performance Fee
    
    Through a myriad of theatre games, improv scenes, and performance opportunities, your child will learn collaboration, confidence, and communication skills in a creative, unique, and innovative environment. An adaptive theatre class open to all abilities and talents. Students may be broken into groups based on age and will be instructed by teachers trained in Communication Sciences and Disorders!
    
    \*\*All students are welcome in all of our classes, and this will not change! But want to offer an option for parents who are in need of a little extra TLC for their students!
    
    Teacher: Jenna Dekich
    
-   ## LC Performance Troupe
    
    **6:15-7:30pm | Ages 9+**
    
    $115/month + $50 performance fee
    
    Have a student who is itching to perform more than once a semester? This class is meant for students that have taken a camp or class with us before, and want to dig deeper into acting. We will start with audition fundamentals, and then cast small groups to put on shows around the community throughout the Spring semester! We will dig deep into characterization through pre-written short plays, scenes and monologues, and learn more advanced improv techniques. Oh, and still have a lot of fun doing so!
    
    Teacher: Hannah Eppling
    

### Thursday Classes

-   ## Intro to Film Creation
    
    **4:30-5:15pm | Ages 6-8**
    
    $85/month + $30 Materials Fee
    
    Does your kid love shorts and want to make their own? This class introduces young performers to all things acting for the camera! Students will explore different genres, storytelling, acting, scriptwriting, and even some camera work!
    
    Teacher: Sunny Cantarella
    
-   ## Film Creation
    
    **5:30-6:30pm | Ages 9+**
    
    $95/month + $30 Materials Fee
    
    Explore acting behind the camera, create short film storylines, and learn how a film comes to life! Students will explore storytelling, scriptwriting, directing, acting, camera work, special effects, and basic editing. Activities will range from storyboarding to mini-film production.
    
    Teacher: Kidd Fielteau
