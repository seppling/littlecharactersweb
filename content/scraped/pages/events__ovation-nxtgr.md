We’re thrilled to partner with Athens First United Methodist Church to offer a monthly class for people with special needs! Hosted in AFUMC’s downtown Athens building, this hour will be spent playing games, allowing exploration through movement and costumes, and really just providing a fun and safe environment for our differently-abled friends and caregivers to enjoy!

This class is donation based and we ask that you sign up at least one day prior to the event so that we can best prepare!

Our Fall 2024 schedule is from 10-11am on:

-   August 17
    
-   September 21
    
-   October 19
    
-   November 16
    
-   December 14
