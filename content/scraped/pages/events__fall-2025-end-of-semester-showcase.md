Join us at our end of semester show “P is for Pom Poms” featuring our ABCs of Theater Class and our two Produce a Show Classes! This showcase will have three one act plays with intermissions between each.

Act 1: The ABCs of Theater

Act 2: Searching for Sa-Squash

Act 3: High School Hospital

**Saturday, December 6, 2025 at 5:30pm at the Marigold Auditorium (373 N Church St, Winterville, GA)**

Doors will open at 5:15 on Saturday the 6th. There will be a raffle open to fundraise for future programming, and food and beverages available for purchase before the show and during intermission! Tickets are $15 - kids 4 and under are free!
