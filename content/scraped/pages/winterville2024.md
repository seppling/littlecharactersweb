## Spring 2025 Winterville Classes

We are excited to offer after-school classes In Winterville on Mondays and Thursdays for students aged 3-17 this Spring from January 13th through May 16th!

Classes are located at the [Winterville Cultural Center](https://www.wintervillecenter.com/) and Marigold Auditorium. A reception area with free coffee and wifi is available to parents!

To enroll in a class, click the below registration link and create an account on our online portal. You’ll be able to see all the classes available to your student once logged in! [Please click here to see our tuition guidelines and discounts!](/s/Little-Characters-Tuition-Guidlines-Spring-2025.pdf) Feel free to reach out if you have any questions!

**The last day to register for Produce a Show classes is** **February 1st** **and the last day to register for all other classes is** **March 1st****!**

### Monday Classes

-   ## Intro to Theater
    
    **4:30-5:15pm | 6-10 Year Olds**
    
    $85/month + $30 performance fee
    
    This course will broaden your Little Characters’ view of all things theater! We will cover vocality, movement, character exploration onstage while working individually and in groups to build confidence, teamwork and individualism!
    
    Teachers: Hannah Eppling, CC Conner
    
-   ## Produce a Show! - 2nd-5th Grade
    
    **5:30 - 6:45pm | 7-10 Year Olds**
    
    $120/month + $50 performance fee
    
    This class is for creative minds and mini producers! We will work with the students to create their own story, turn it into a script, and spend the semester rehearsing to bring it to life at the end of the semester.
    
    Teachers: CC Conner, Hannah Eppling
    
-   ## Produce a Show! - 6th-12th Grade
    
    **6:45-8:00pm | 11-17 Year Olds**
    
    $120/month + $50 performance fee
    
    This class is for creative minds and mini producers! We will work with the students to create their own story, turn it into a script, and spend the semester rehearsing to bring it to life at the end of the semester.
    
    Teachers: CC Conner, Hannah Eppling
    

### Thursday Classes

-   ## Film 101
    
    **5:30-6:30pm | 8+ Years Old**
    
    $100/month + $30 Materials Fee
    
    Explore acting behind the camera, create short film storylines, and learn how a film comes to life! Students will explore storytelling, scriptwriting, directing, acting, camera work, special effects, and basic editing. Activities will range from storyboarding to mini-film production.
    
    Teacher: Kidd Fielteau
