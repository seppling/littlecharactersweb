Remember your favorite fairy tales? Yes, well, we took those and jazzed 'em up a bit by letting our 11-17 year old students retell them THEIR way. With interpretations of Rapunzel, Billy Goats Gruff, and Hansel and Gretel, this night holds a story for everyone!

We've loved each and every minute spent with our Little Characters students this semester in our biggest semester yet! We can not wait to show you all of the hard work, creativity and just pure joy these kids have put into the shows.

This show will have 3 original performances (at around 15 mins each!) with intermissions in-between! Snacks provided and drinks for purchase!

May 3rd - 6:30PM

Tickets are $13/person - kids 3 and under are free!
