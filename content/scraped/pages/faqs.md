## Frequently Asked Questions

####   
Where are you located?

We are right on W. Broad street in between Rocksprings and Alps! Our address is 1635 and is located on the South Side of Braod. St. We are grateful to have a 5000 square ft. accessible space that is bursting with color and natural light! Reach out if you’d like to tour it! In the meantime, here is a handy dandy [Parking Guide](/s/Little-Characters-Parking-Instructions.pdf). In January 2026, our private back lot will be open so that you and your little characters can enter through the back of the building and will not have to walk on Broad!

#### Who do you serve?

We offer camps, classes and workshops to students in grades pre-K - 5th through Little Characters, and students in grades 6-12 and adults through [Geode](/geode)! All of our teachers are trained to give the most caring theater education to folks of all backgrounds and abilities, and is our hope to serve anyone who may be interested in joining! Some of our students have been acting for years and some are tip toeing on stage for the first time. It’s our hope to create a sense of belonging for everyone.

Inclusivity is one of our core values, and we are proud to partner with the UGA Speech and Hearing clinic to provide 1-1 support to students with higher needs at no cost to parents.

#### How Much Do Your Classes Cost?

It is our hope to offer classes at a fair rate to our families while also being able to pay our staff well! Our typical breakdown is:

-   45 minute classes run at $85 per month
    
-   1-hour classes run at $95 per month
    
-   1.25 hour classes run at $115 per month
    
-   1.5 hour classes run at $125 per month
    

Each class has a one-time performance fee that goes toward paying teachers for extra writing/rehearsal time, costumes, props, and facility rentals.

We also host camps at around $50/day, and workshops that vary in cost based on hours/materials needed. We hope to soon offer a monthly Free Community Class and are continually working on fundraising to support students that need adjusted rates. More details on our Financial Guidelines can be found [here](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/61fc0808d7415f6a4cd36b0a/t/68b71c39ddce5541be8209b7/1756830777258/Little+Characters+Theater+Troupe+Tuition+Guidelines+-+2025+%281%29.pdf). _Please reach out to us if you are in need of flexible rates, we never want cost to prohibit students from joining our programs!_

#### What are your Core Values?

● **Integrity:** How we treat each other, our people, our community and our performances

● **Connection:** Building lasting relationships and trust with each other and our students

● **Professionalism:** Uphold our mission and treating each other with fidelity while working as a team to allow students to be a part of something meaningful

● **Flexibility:** Having each other's backs, pivoting when needed, taking breaks and regrouping

● **Fun:** Not taking ourselves too seriously, being ok with messing up, encouraging each other’s creativity

#### Do you Offer Parties?

Heck yeah we do! Name the occasion, and we’ll make it happen! We are happy to offer two hour parties on evenings or weekends at our space. This includes customizable theater games and content, your own Teacher/Party Coordinator, a stage for dance parties and present opening, and tables and chairs for cake and snacks! Don’t tempt us with a good time. Or, do! ;) [Email us](mailto:littlecharacterstheater@gmail.com?subject=Party%20Inquiry) for more information.

#### How big are your classes and camps?

Our classes tend to be around 8-12 kids, and our camps and workshops tend to be around 12-20. We have a ratio of 8 students to 1 teacher, and will add more teachers if any of our friends need a little extra attention. We must have 5 students for a class or camp to make, and we try our best to let our families know ahead of time if events need to be adjusted accordingly.
