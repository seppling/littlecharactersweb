# **Our Team**

**Little Characters Theater Troupe** is made up of an incredible group of creative individuals. We are so grateful for what each of these brilliant folks bring to our little corner of the world! It is an honor and a privilege to work together to deliver an extra special experience to our community!

* * *

-   #### Hannah Eppling
    
    Owner + Creative Director + Teacher
    
    Hi! I’m Hannah Eppling, and I’m so happy you are here! I have a yearning to encourage the hearts of others - and my favorite way to do so is through theater. When I started directing my sister in our basement at the age of four, my parents knew they needed to get me in drama classes ASAP. I was in my first play at the age of eight, and continued on through high school, college and professional work on stage, film and improvisation.
    
    In 2016, I made the move from my 9-5 job to start apprenticing in theater education. Over the past eight years, I’ve taught theater to 3-50 year olds and started Little Characters in order to bring more play and creativity to the Athens community! I am trained in theater for social change and programming for all ages and abilities.
    
    I have a strong desire to grow lasting relationships and influence my students in innovative, atypical ways. My favorite teaching topics are emotional development, improv and characterization on stage. I believe theater is a beautiful way to grow and share the gifts we were born with. When not working, I love getting outside with my husband, Stephen and kiddos, Colette and Everett.
    
    [Click here](https://www.littlecharacters.org/s/Hannah-Eppling-Experience-and-Teaching-Philosophy-1.pdf) for full bio and teaching philosophy.

-   #### Carley Pedan
    
    Teacher
    
    Carley is a lover of all arts, and she holds a special place in her heart for theater. She took drama classes all four years of high school, where she gained experience playing a variety of characters, constructing sets, writing scripts, stage managing, acting competitively, and student directing. She participated in over fifteen productions at Statesboro High, but her favorite roles include Wednesday from The Addams Family, Dr. Vivian Bearing from Wit, and Dragon from Shrek. Carley’s musical roots come from playing guitar and singing in youth band group School of Rock and Roll for seven years, where she developed a passion for performing. Now a student at the University of Georgia, she is pursuing a major in Landscape Architecture and a certificate in Music Business. Inspired by her First-Year Odyssey seminar, “Theatre for Change,” Carley is excited to share the power of drama to shape lives and build confidence with Little Characters students!

    Operations Manager

-   #### Emily Greene
    
    Set, Costume and Prop Designer
    
    Emily is a set and costume designer that has worked on Broadway productions, regional theater and film. She has a BFA in Theatre from the University of Montevallo, and an MFA in Set and Costume design from Ohio University. She designed 75+ productions as a freelance set and costume designer in the San Francisco Bay Area, and spent 5 years as a set designer at the stop motion film company Laika Studios where she worked on 3 Academy award nominated films, and was nominated for 2 VES awards. She is now the mother of a 9 year old little character.

-   #### Zack Newcott
    
    Lead Teacher
    
    Also known as "Mr. Zack" in many classrooms across the county, Zack Newcott has worked as a long-term substitute teacher in Athens for several years, led adventures piloting boats and warding off hippos as a trusted skipper on Disneyland's Jungle Cruise, and appeared as colorful characters — even Prince Charming himself — in local productions from Bethesda, MD, to Visalia, CA.
    
    Somewhere in between, he's written a humor column ("The Newcott Scoop") as a USA Today reporter, created the animated series "Humorous Vacuum Robot" (now streaming on Hulu's "Comedy Dynamics: Coming to the Stage"), published two novels, and racked up a couple of short films and episodic credits along the way.
    
    He holds a degree in Film/Screenwriting from Biola University, is GACE certified through UGA for grades K–12 in English and Mathematics, and is a Georgia Certified Peer Counselor with GMHCN. But his most rigorous training, he'll happily admit, comes courtesy of his wife and kids.
