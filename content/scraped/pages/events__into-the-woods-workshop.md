Get ready for a magical morning of singing, dancing, acting, and so much fun!

The talented cast of UGA Next Act’s production of Into the Woods will be leading a workshop in partnership with Little Characters! Participants will learn a song and dance straight from the show, work through scenes from the production, and receive feedback from working actors. It’s an incredible opportunity for young performers to build confidence, grow their skills, and experience the magic of musical theater up close!
