Come and enjoy the hard work our students have done over the semester at our Variety Show! There will be Improv, there will be Short Films, there will be costumes, and so much more! All at the Marigold Auditorium in Winterville, GA.

Saturday, November 15th at 6pm and Sunday, November 16th at 3pm.

Featuring our Kids Improv Class, Scenes and Monologues Class, Film 101 Class and Private Lessons Students! Tickets are $12 a person (ages 4 and under are free!) and can be purchased below!

There will be snacks and drinks (leaded and unleaded) for purchase during the show and at intermission. We can’t wait to see you there!
