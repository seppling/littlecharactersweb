Little Characters Performance Troupe presents Oliver Twist!  
_Oliver Twist_ is a dramatic stage adaptation of Charles Dickens’ classic novel, focusing on the orphan Oliver’s journey through 19th-century London’s criminal underworld. It features key characters like Fagin, the Artful Dodger, Bill Sikes, and Nancy, highlighting themes of mystery, betrayal, and ultimate redemption.  
Come see this classic story performed on May 1st at 6 PM or May 2nd at 1 PM at the Marigold Auditorium!
