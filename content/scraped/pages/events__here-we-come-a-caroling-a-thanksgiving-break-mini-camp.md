Little Characters presents our cheeriest, holiday packed mini camp ever! Come join us for 3 half days packed with a ton of fun. Each day we will practice a song and dance, act out holiday jingles as if they were scenes from a play, play some super fun Holiday Themed games (Turkey Trott anyone?) and do an art activity. This mini camp will conclude with a performance on 11/22 at 12PM. We will show off our amazing skills with everything we have been working on during the camp. Cookies and Cocoa to follow performance.

**_The Important Information:_** 

**_Ages: 4 - 12_**

**_When: 11/20 - 11/22/2023_**

**_Where: Winterville Cultural Arts Center_** 

**_Cost: $150 or $50/day_** 

**_Performance: 11/22/2023 12PM_**
