Come spend the Monday afternoon of Thanksgiving Break with your favorite LC teachers, friends, and a whole bunch of cardboard, paint and glue! This workshop will be all hands on while students learn how to create the props (and set pieces!) for our December shows!! 

Everyone who attends the workshop will receive a free ticket to the December show and have their name put into the program (and also bragging rights forever as they see their creations under the stage lights!) 

  
We ask our students to wear closed toed shoes and clothes they can get messy! Nut-free snacks will be provided, but please bring a water bottle! 

The Important Information: 

**Ages: 6+**

**When: 11/24 from 1-4pm**

**Where: 1635 W. Broad St. Athens, GA 30606 in the Downstairs Art Studio!**

**Cost: $20**
