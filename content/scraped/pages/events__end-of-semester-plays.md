At LC, our bread and butter is working with students to produce their own shows, and this year’s classes will not disappoint! Hijinks, mystery and big laughs are in store at our final shows of the semester!

Performance Times:

-   Saturday, December 14 at 6pm
    
    -   Our Intro to Theater class will open up with a show all about Holidays in Florida - singing, dancing and cuteness will abound.
        
    -   Our Elementary Produce a Show class will send us laughing in a fantasy rom-com
        
    -   Our Middle School Produce a Show class will turn up the heat with a holiday who-dunnit that is sure to keep you on your tows! Did anyone say murder mystery based on your favorite holiday meal dishes?!
        
-   Sunday, December 15 at 3pm
    
    -   Our ABCs of Theater class will open up with a show all about Holidays in Florida - these 4-6 year old are sure to light up the room brighter than a palm-tree with Christmas lights!
        
    -   Our Elementary Produce a Show class will send us laughing in a fantasy rom-com
        
    -   Our Middle School Produce a Show class will turn up the heat with a holiday who-dunnit that is sure to keep you on your tows! Did anyone say murder mystery based on your favorite holiday meal dishes?!
        

All shows are one act and there will be an intermission between each show with food, drinks and a fundraising raffle! Tickets are $15 a piece (kids 4 and under are free!)

DON’T FORGET to dress in your favorite tropical or holiday attire!!
