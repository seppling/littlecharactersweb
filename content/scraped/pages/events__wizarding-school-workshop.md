_You're invited on a magical adventure. While school is out, come to our Wizarding School Workshop and experience a world of wonder._ 

_We will have Potions Class where will we make Butter Beer and a tasty treat. Herbology where we will learn our Patronus and get crafty with pumpkins. Defense Against the Dark Arts where our theatrics will shine. Rounding out our class offerings we will spend some time playing with game of Quidditch._ 

_You will leave the day feel like you have just spent a full day at the famous Wizarding School from all the movies._ 

_Costumes Encouraged!_
