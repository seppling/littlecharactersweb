Valentines Day is right around the corner and it's on a Saturday this year! Looks like the stars are aligning for you to have that romantic evening out, a galentines night to remember, or that self care night you have been waiting for. Only one thing is missing, someone to look after the kids!

Look no further! We are hosting a Valentines Day Parents Night Out for Little Characters ages 4 and up. We will have Valentine's crafts, theater games, pizza, and so much fun for everyone!

We ask our students to wear closed toed shoes and clothes they can move around in! Please bring a water bottle and a snack if you want (no nuts please! We will be providing pizza.) Please let us know below if your student has any allergies! 

**The Important Information:** 

**Ages: 4+**

**When: February 14th from 5-9pm**

**Where: 1635 W. Broad St. Athens GA 30606**

**Cost: $40 per family**
