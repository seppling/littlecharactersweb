## 9. Reviews

Reviews decide who rises in the map results, and they decide whether a parent taps through. Read [Google's tips](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7035772) and the [review rules](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114).

**Google's rules, in short.**

1. **Ask every family.** Don't pick only the happy ones. Google bans "selectively soliciting positive reviews".
2. **Offer nothing in return**: no discount, no free class, no raffle. That includes asking someone to remove or change a bad review in return for a favor.
3. Don't ask for specific wording, or for a specific staff member to be named. Don't give staff a quota.
4. Don't set up a review station: no tablet or device at the space for people to write reviews on. A printed QR code that families scan on their own phones is fine (see below).

**How to get your review link.** Open your profile while signed in. Tap **Ask for reviews** (also called "Get more reviews" or "Share review form"). Copy the link. If you don't see it, follow [Google's tips](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7035772).

**Review request: email**

> Subject: How was your time at Little Characters?
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Thank you for being part of [the Variety Show / camp week / this term]. If you have a minute, we would love to hear about your family's experience. Honest reviews help other Athens families find us, whatever you thought.
>
> You can leave a review here: [link]
>
> Thank you,
> Hannah

**Review request: text**

> Hi [first name], it's Hannah at Little Characters. Thanks for being part of [camp this week / our show]. If you have a minute, an honest Google review helps other families find us: [link]

**When to send.** Send it to **every** family within a few days after:

- the Variety Show (December and spring),
- each camp week (on the Friday or Monday after),
- Parents' Night Out and the end of the term.

Send it to families who attended, not people who haven't been. Keep one list so no family gets it more than once a term.

**QR code at shows.** Make a QR code from the review link with a free generator (for example, search for "free QR code generator"). Print it on the show program or a sign by the door with the words "Share your experience on Google". Don't hand out anything for scanning it, and don't stand by it asking people.

**Reply templates.** Reply to every review within a few days. Use the person's first name only. Never share anything about a child (class, age, behavior) in a reply, even for a positive review.

*Glowing review*

> Thank you, [first name]! Messages like this mean a lot to our whole team. We hope to see you on stage again soon. — Hannah

*Mixed review*

> Thank you for your honest feedback, [first name]. We're glad [the good part] went well and we're sorry [the problem]. We'd like to make this right. Please email us at littlecharacterstheater@gmail.com. — Hannah

*Negative review*

> We're sorry your experience wasn't what you hoped, [first name]. Every family matters to us and we'd like to understand what happened. Please email us at littlecharacterstheater@gmail.com or call (281) 798-2623. — Hannah

If a review is fake, abusive or about a different business, you can flag it for removal from the review itself ([manage reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050)). Don't argue in public.
